# **LEVIN – Space Biology Knowledge Engine**

### *XOTeam • NASA Space Apps Challenge Winner — 3rd Place (Amman) 2025 • Globally Nominated*

---

## 🚀 **Summary**

**LEVIN** is a space biology knowledge engine dedicated to exploring the biological challenges of space.
Named in honor of **Gilbert V. Levin**, who led NASA’s *Viking Labeled Release Experiment (1976)*—the first direct life-detection test on Mars—LEVIN embodies the courage to ask bold scientific questions.

NASA has conducted decades of space biology research, yet the findings are scattered across hundreds of publications, making insight extraction slow and difficult. **LEVIN solves this challenge** by unifying NASA’s data into a centralized, intelligent, and interactive platform.

The solution blends **AI-driven analysis**, **visual dashboards**, **3D models**, and a **mobile-first experience** designed for both beginners and researchers. The platform benefits:

* **Scientists** generating new hypotheses
* **Mission architects** planning for safe Moon & Mars exploration
* **Decision-makers & investors** identifying research gaps and opportunities

## 🧬 **Project Details**

LEVIN is a **Space Biology Knowledge Engine** designed to address one problem:
NASA’s biological research is *vast, scattered,* and difficult to filter.

The project consolidates this research into an interactive, intelligent, and easily searchable platform.

---

## 🛠️ **How LEVIN Works**

LEVIN functions as a multi-layered system composed of the following core components:

### **🔸 Centralized Knowledge Hub**

A unified archive of NASA’s scattered space biology studies, research insights, and categorized themes.

### **🔸 AI-Powered Chatbot**

A smart assistant that:

* Answers scientific questions
* Generates concise summaries from NASA publications
* Provides real-time insights using AI/API integrations

### **🔸 AI Agent (Behind the Scenes)**

Used during development to:

* Process NASA data
* Extract relevant content
* Map research gaps and opportunities
* Automate manual scientific analysis

### **🔸 Dashboards & Visual Tools**

Includes:

* Scientific charts
* Gap Bubble Maps
* Interactive visuals for research exploration

### **🔸 3D Interactive Models**

Allows users to visualize:

* Cells
* Microbes
* Plants
* Radiation effects
* Biological responses in space

### **🔸 Mobile-First Experience**

A streamlined interface for beginners and students with:

* Simplified exploration tools
* A **Researchers Verify Tool** (validation assistant using the AI Agent)

## 🧩 **Challenge Statement (NASA Space Apps)**

**Build a Space Biology Knowledge Engine**

NASA needs a dynamic tool that summarizes bioscience publications using AI, knowledge graphs, and visualization techniques—allowing stakeholders to explore experimental impacts and scientific results related to human spaceflight.

LEVIN directly fulfills this challenge by creating a centralized, intelligent, and interactive research engine.
